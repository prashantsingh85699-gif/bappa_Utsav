## 🌟 Overview & Visual Experience

Bappa Utsav immerses players into the grand sanctum of a Ganesh Chaturthi celebration:
- **Live Utsav Stage**: Decorated golden mandap, central respectful Ganesha focal area, floating flower petals, flickering oil diyas, layered garlands, and warm ambient light rays.
- **Unified Progression**: Earn sacred festival points across all activities to advance from **Level 1 (Aagman)** up to **Level 6 (Morya Legend)**, unlocking exclusive mandap decorations along the way.
- **Zero-Distraction Responsive Design**: Custom-tuned for Android mobile browsers (360x800, 375x812, 390x844, 412x915) as well as desktop displays.

## 🎮 How to Play All Games (Full Guide & Controls)

### 🥁 1. Dhol Beat (Rhythm Highway)
*Experience the thunderous energy of Pune & Nashik Dhol-Tasha pathaks!*

- **Objective**: Hit rhythm notes precisely as they cross the golden strike zone to build the festival beat and rack up high combos.
- **Controls**:
  - **Desktop Keyboard**: Keys `[D]`, `[F]`, `[J]`, `[K]` corresponding to the 4 rhythm lanes.
  - **Mobile / Touch**: Tap the 4 responsive brass touch pads at the bottom of the screen.
- **Judgement & Scoring**:
  - `PERFECT` (+100 pts + streak bonus)
  - `GREAT` (+70 pts)
  - `GOOD` (+40 pts)
  - `MISS` (Resets combo streak)
- **Pro Tip**: Keep an unbroken combo going to activate up to **5x Score Multipliers**!

---

### 🌸 2. Mandap Designer (Altar Sandbox)
*Decorate Lord Ganesha’s sacred altar with traditional Indian ornamentation.*

- **Objective**: Arrange flowers, royal brass samais, glowing diyas, velvet curtains, torans, and prasad offerings to create an auspicious mandap.
- **Controls**:
  - **Add Items**: Select items from the bottom drawer categorized by *Diyas*, *Flowers*, *Garlands*, *Offerings*, and *Curtains*.
  - **Drag & Position**: Touch and drag on mobile, or click and drag on desktop.
  - **Controls Bar**:
    - **Rotate**: Rotate items 15° clockwise or counter-clockwise.
    - **Scale**: Enlarge or shrink items for depth.
    - **Layering**: Bring forward / send backward.
    - **Delete / Reset**: Remove individual items or clear the canvas.
- **Divine Harmony Score**: Evaluates variety, symmetry, lighting balance, and sacred offerings (up to 5,000 pts).
- **Save Mandap**: Saves your unique pandal to your devotee profile so it greets you every time!

---

### 🥟 3. Modak Catch (Arcade Reflex)
*Catch delicious modaks and sacred sweets while dodging firecracker hazards!*

- **Objective**: Move the golden offering basket to collect falling festive delicacies within the 45-second timer.
- **Controls**:
  - **Mobile Touch**: Drag the basket smoothly across the bottom of the screen.
  - **Desktop Mouse**: Move mouse left/right to glide the basket.
  - **Desktop Keyboard**: Arrow keys (`←`, `→`) or `[A]` and `[D]`.
- **Items & Points**:
  - 🥟 **Steamed Modak**: +100 pts
  - ✨ **Golden Modak**: +250 pts
  - 🍬 **Motichoor Laddoo**: +150 pts
  - 🍌 **Sacred Banana**: +80 pts
  - 🌶️ **Spicy Chili**: -1 Life / resets streak
  - 🎆 **Firecracker**: -1 Life & basket stun
- **Lives**: You start with 3 sacred lives. Catching 10 modaks in a row grants a **Golden Immunity Shield**!

---

### 🧠 4. Bappa Quiz (Sacred Lore & Trivia)
*Test your cultural wisdom on the traditions, history, and symbolism of Ganeshotsav.*

- **Objective**: Answer 10 culturally respectful questions per round.
- **Features**:
  - 25+ curated questions covering Lokmanya Tilak’s 1893 initiative, the symbolism of Bappa's large ears, the mouse (Mooshak) vahana, the single tusk, modak significance, and eco-friendly clay celebrations.
  - Immediate visual feedback with swara sound effects.
  - Detailed wisdom card explanations after every question.
- **Scoring**: Base points + speed bonus + streak multipliers for consecutive correct answers.

### 🔥 5. Daily Festival Challenge & Group Rooms
*Compete with fellow devotees on rotating daily targets or create private family rooms!*

- **Daily Challenge**:
  - Rotates every midnight (e.g. *Day 1: Nashik Dhol Mahotsav*, *Day 2: Sacred Altar Harmony*, *Day 3: Golden Modak Rush*, *Day 4: Divine Lore Quiz*).
  - Countdown timer until midnight.
  - Target score to unlock exclusive daily badges.

  - Share the code with friends or family.
  - Other devotees click **"Join Festival Room"** and enter the code to compete on a private group leaderboard!

## 🛡️ Devotee Authentication & Official Leaderboard

### Verified Devotees System
To prevent fake, unknown, or arbitrary score spam, Bappa Utsav includes a built-in **Credential Authentication System**:
1. **Sign Up / Log In**:
   - Click the **"Verify"** button on the Header or Home screen.
   - Enter your Devotee Name (Nickname), Username/Email, Passcode, and favorite avatar.
   - Once authenticated, you receive a golden **`✓ VERIFIED`** badge.
2. **Official Verified Leaderboard**:
   - By default, the leaderboard operates in **"Official Verified Devotees Only"** mode.
   - Only scores earned by authenticated players appear on the public board.
3. **Host Controls (🧹 Purge & Clean)**:
   - On the Leaderboard screen, click the **Sliders/Controls icon (`⚙️`)** on the top right.
   - **"Purge Mock / Demo Users"**: Clears any sample mock devotees with 1 click so only real players remain.
   - **"Full Reset for Launch"**: Wipes all test scores clean so launch day starts fresh.

## 🧪 Complete Testing & QA Guide

### How to Test Everything Locally:
1. **Start the Development Server**:
   ```bash
   npm run dev
   ```
   Open `http://localhost:3000/` in Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge.

2. **Test Flow & Navigation**:
   - **Splash Screen**: Click anywhere or tap to prime audio and enter the Home Screen.
   - **Verify Account**: Tap the **"Verify"** button on top. Test creating an account with a username and passcode.
   - **Festival Hub**: Tap **"Start Utsav"** to enter the Hub.
   - **Play Mini-Games**:
     - Play **Dhol Beat** (use keys `D`, `F`, `J`, `K` or touch pads).
     - Decorate in **Mandap Designer** and tap "Save Mandap".
     - Catch modaks in **Modak Catch** (use Arrow keys or drag mouse/finger).
     - Test your knowledge in **Bappa Quiz**.
   - **Test Leaderboard**:
     - Navigate to **Ranks / Leaderboard**.
     - Verify your score appears with the golden `✓ VERIFIED` badge.
     - Toggle the **"Official Verified Devotees Only"** filter.
     - Click **Controls (`⚙️`)** to test "Purge Mock / Demo Users".
   - **Test Maha Aarti**:
     - Complete all 4 games to trigger the grand completion ceremony.

3. **Test Mobile Viewports**:
   - In Chrome DevTools (Press `F12`), toggle Device Toolbar (`Ctrl + Shift + M`).
   - Test standard mobile dimensions:
     - `360 x 800` (Standard Android)
     - `375 x 812` (iPhone X / 11 / 12 mini)
     - `390 x 844` (iPhone 12 / 13 / 14)
     - `412 x 915` (Samsung Galaxy / Pixel)
   - Verify that all buttons are finger-friendly, text does not overflow, and no horizontal scrollbar appears.

4. **Test Production Build**:
   ```bash
   npm run build
   npm run preview
   ```
   Ensures bundle optimization and clean compilation with zero warnings.

## 🌐 Deployment Guide (Vercel, Netlify, Firebase)

Bappa Utsav is a modern static web application that can be hosted on any web host for free:

### 1. Deploy to Vercel (Recommended — 2 Minutes):
1. Sign up/log in at [https://vercel.com](https://vercel.com) with GitHub.
2. Click **"Add New Project"** and import **`prashantsingh85699-gif/bappa_Utsav`**.
3. Framework preset will automatically be detected as **Vite**.
4. Click **"Deploy"**.
5. Your live URL (e.g. `https://bappa-utsav.vercel.app`) is generated instantly!

### 2. Deploy to Netlify:
1. Go to [https://netlify.com](https://netlify.com).
2. Click **"Add new site"** -> **"Import from Git"** -> select repo.
3. Build command: `npm run build` | Publish directory: `dist`.
4. Click **"Deploy site"**.

### 3. Optional: Firebase Cloud Firestore Leaderboard
The game works 100% offline-first out of the box. To connect a cross-device Firebase cloud database, add your Firebase keys to a `.env` file or in your hosting provider's dashboard:
```env
VITE_FIREBASE_API_KEY=your-api-key
VITE_FIREBASE_AUTH_DOMAIN=your-project.firebaseapp.com
VITE_FIREBASE_PROJECT_ID=your-project-id
VITE_FIREBASE_STORAGE_BUCKET=your-project.appspot.com
VITE_FIREBASE_MESSAGING_SENDER_ID=your-sender-id
VITE_FIREBASE_APP_ID=your-app-id
```
